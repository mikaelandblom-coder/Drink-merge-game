Wiring board — which sound each map plays

Every map × every event. Pick a sound from the dropdown to hear it immediately; ▶ replays it the way the game fires it (a merge plays the map's whole chain as a combo cascade, a collision plays soft → hard, coins play a full payout shower). Default means the map inherits the top row, so changing the top row moves every map that hasn't opted out. Then hit Save and bump the ?v= in index.html.

Sound library — audition & assign

Every voice in config/sounds.js, grouped by the event it can be wired to. Auditions use the tier chain of the map picked below, so a merge sound is judged with the right number of tiers and the real item names. When Mai says "the farm merge sound is annoying": set the map here, play the alternatives, hit Use for… on the winner, Save.
